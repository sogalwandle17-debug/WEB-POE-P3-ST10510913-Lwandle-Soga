Welcome to Our Center

The Tshwane Community Youth Center is a safe space for young people in Pretoria. Our goal is to empower youth through education, skills, and support.

Our Main Programs:

  • After-School Tutoring: Free help with Maths, Science and English
  • Skills Development: Computer skills, CV writing and job readiness
  • Wellness Support: Counseling and a safe space to talk

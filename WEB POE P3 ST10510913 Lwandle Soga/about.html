The Tshwane Community Youth Centre was formed 5 years ago in order to help the youth in the Pretoria West area. The centre provides free after-school activities, computer classes and job assistance to youth between the ages of 14 and 25. The centre was started by community members who saw that many young people needed support after school.

Mission

Empower youth through education, skills training and community involvement.

Vision

To have a community in which all youth get the opportunity to learn and prosper.

Target Audience

High school learners, unemployed youth, parents and community donors in the Pretoria West area who want to support youth development.
